# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 8 September 2026**
**Effective: 8 September 2026**

This policy applies to the KnowFlow website at `www.knowflowchat.cn` (the "Website"), operated by **合肥知识库流动人工智能应用软件有限责任公司** ("we", "us").

We take your personal information seriously. Please read this policy before using the Website. **If you do not agree with any part of it, please do not use the enquiry form.**

:::info In one sentence
This is a product and documentation website. Unless you choose to fill in and submit the enquiry form, we collect no personally identifying information from you. The Website uses no advertising cookies and performs no cross-site tracking.
:::

---

## 1. What we collect

### 1.1 Information you provide

We only collect the following when you actively submit the form on the [Contact page](/contact):

| Field | Required | Why we collect it |
|---|---|---|
| Name | Yes | To address you and establish contact |
| Company | Yes | To understand your context and prepare a relevant proposal |
| WeChat ID | Yes | To reach you |
| Email | No | To send product material and proposal documents |
| Job title | No | To pitch the conversation at the right technical depth |
| Enquiry type | Yes | To route you to the right pre-sales or technical colleague |
| Message | No | To prepare a relevant demo in advance |

**All of it is voluntary.** Leaving optional fields blank does not affect our ability to contact you.

### 1.2 Information collected automatically

Server logs record ordinary access data — IP address, browser type and version, operating system, timestamp, pages visited and referrer. This supports security and traffic statistics and **is not linked to your identity**.

### 1.3 Browser storage

The Website uses `localStorage` for interface preferences such as sidebar state. This data **stays in your browser and is never uploaded to our servers**.

## 2. How we use it

We use the information we collect only to:

1. **Respond to your enquiry** — contact you with a demo, technical consultation, pricing or partnership discussion;
2. **Prepare relevant material** based on your company size and use case;
3. **Improve our products** by analysing common needs in aggregated, de-identified form.

:::warning What we will not do
- We will **not** sell, rent or trade your personal information to any third party;
- We will **not** use it for purposes not stated in this policy;
- We will **not** send you marketing unrelated to your enquiry without your consent.
:::

---

## 3. How information flows and is stored

### 3.1 Where form submissions go

Form content is delivered to our internal work group through a **WeCom (Enterprise WeChat) group bot webhook**, where a pre-sales or technical colleague picks it up.

This means the content passes through **Tencent's WeCom servers**. Tencent's handling of that data is governed by its own policy — see the [Tencent Privacy Policy](https://privacy.qq.com/).

### 3.2 Retention

- **Enquiry form data**: retained for **24 months** after our commercial conversation ends, to support follow-up service and contract performance, then deleted or anonymised;
- **Server access logs**: retained for **6 months**;
- Where law mandates a different retention period, that period applies.

### 3.3 Security measures

- The Website is served over **HTTPS** throughout;
- The internal work group is restricted to authorised colleagues;
- Colleagues who can access your information are bound by confidentiality obligations.

:::caution
No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. We apply reasonable commercial effort but cannot guarantee absolute security. **Please do not put passwords, ID numbers or bank account details into the message field.**
:::

## 4. Third-party services

| Service | Provider | Purpose | Data involved |
|---|---|---|---|
| WeCom group bot | Tencent | Receives enquiry submissions | The form content you submit |
| Baidu Search Resource Platform link submission | Baidu | Submits page URLs so Baidu can index them | Current page URL, referrer |
| jsDelivr CDN | jsDelivr | Serves some images used in blog posts | IP address, browser information |

:::info About the Baidu link submission script
The Website loads Baidu's link submission script (`push.js`). It submits **the current page URL** to Baidu so content is indexed faster. **It does not collect personally identifying information and is not used for advertising.**
:::

The Website uses **no** ad networks, user profiling or cross-site tracking services.

## 5. Your rights

Under the Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China and related regulations, you have the right to:

- **Access** — find out what information we hold about you;
- **Correct** — have inaccurate or incomplete information fixed;
- **Delete** — request deletion of your personal information;
- **Withdraw consent** — at any time;
- **Complain** — about how we handle personal information.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details in [section 7](#7-contact-us). We respond **within 15 working days** of receiving a request.

We may ask for information necessary to verify your identity before acting on a request.

This is a business-to-business product site and is **not directed at minors**. We do not knowingly collect information from minors. If you are a guardian and believe a minor has submitted information to us, please contact us to have it deleted.

We may update this policy from time to time and will change the "Last updated" date at the top of the page. **For material changes affecting your rights, we will post a prominent notice on the Website.**

This policy covers **the Website only**.

Data handling inside the product is governed by the commercial contract between us.
